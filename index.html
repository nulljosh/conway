Toroid

The Game of Life on every screen you own. Draw a pattern, press play, and let four rules do the rest.

Play in the browser How it works

Four rules, nothing else

Every cell looks at its eight neighbours, and the whole board updates at once. That is the entire game — there is no scoring, no goal and no opponent.

  • Underpopulation. A live cell with fewer than two live neighbours dies.
  • Survival. A live cell with two or three live neighbours lives on.
  • Overcrowding. A live cell with more than three live neighbours dies.
  • Reproduction. A dead cell with exactly three live neighbours comes alive.

The board wraps at the edges, so a glider that walks off the right side comes back on the left.

What it looks like

No accounts, no network

Toroid runs entirely on your device. There is no sign-in, no analytics and nothing to collect, because the game is a few hundred lines of arithmetic and never needs to talk to a server.

Install it anywhere.

Toroid runs on every platform. On Windows, Linux and Android it installs straight from the browser — no store, no download, no runtime. It gets a real window, its own icon, and keeps working offline.
